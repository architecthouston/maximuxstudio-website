Help us help you

Including these details in your message lets us look into the issue faster:

  • The app name (for example, RBT Quest)
  • Your device model and operating system version
  • The app version, if you know it
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen
  • Screenshots, if they help explain the issue

Please don't include passwords or other sensitive information in your email.
